# Product Context

## Why This Exists
To give children aged 7–10 a fun, engaging, and self-contained math practice tool they can use independently on a tablet without needing a teacher present. Instant feedback helps children learn immediately from mistakes.

## Problems Solved
- Children need repetitive practice to build arithmetic fluency
- Traditional worksheets provide no immediate feedback
- Needs to work on a tablet without a physical keyboard

## How It Works
1. Child taps a math topic (Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division, or Mixed)
2. A question is shown: `a ○ b = ?`
3. Child taps digits on the on-screen number pad to enter their answer
4. Child taps "Check ✅" for instant feedback
5. Correct → 🎉 celebration; Wrong → 😅 with correct answer shown
6. Child taps "Next →" to continue
7. After 10 questions → Round Summary showing score and question-by-question review
8. Child can "Play Again" with the same topic or go "Home" to pick a new topic

## UX Goals
- Engaging and colourful — children should enjoy using it
- Large fonts and touch targets — no precision required
- No text input — number-only pad prevents frustration
- Immediate positive/negative feedback with emojis
- No timers or pressure — just learning at their own pace
